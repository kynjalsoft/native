## Changes made in the client

- Staff push resolve accepts only a `MESSAGE` response with a current JMAP email. JMAP supplies the current thread; unresolved references do not navigate.
- A foreground rich alert checks the relay-supplied opaque registration ID against the active staff registration, consent, and session. Legacy rich payloads become generic local alerts retaining the reference; explicit registration mismatches are suppressed. Taps still require authenticated exact-message resolution.
- Defaulted staff message previews to on for new installations, matching the requested experience. Preview registration and ACTIVE status require `previewMode: "sender-subject-snippet-v1"` from live relay health. Generic registration is permitted only with previews off. Successful registration also gates foreground preview display in this process.
- Set the generic Android channel to default importance, private lock-screen visibility and no badge. A preview-capable relay uses the separate `mail-messages-v2` channel. Android retains user choices and much of a channel's original behavior once created, so upgrade devices require explicit observation.
- Synchronized the global email switch and company push UI; hid irrelevant calendar controls on company accounts; cleared already visible company alerts on revoke and preview opt-out.
- Staff device consent is stored per local account ID. The secure account-to-subject association authenticates ownership and migrates older subject preferences only when one saved account can be proven to own the subject. Ambiguous or unowned legacy consent requires a fresh opt-in. Removing one staff account does not clear another account's independent consent, even when both share a subject.
- Scoped Android local push to one unambiguous account; separated native notification IDs and dedupe records by both local and JMAP account; excluded current junk/trash/drafts/sent roles; saved successful posts one by one. Propagated the JMAP account through the native tap intent for shared mailboxes. Account opt-out/removal clears delivered Android mail cards, and the headless task checks account ownership and preview preference around posting.
- Captured native Android taps before auth restoration and delayed navigation until unlock and navigator readiness. Taps without a local account ID are ignored. Prior-build per-message taps without a JMAP account ID search the authenticated local account's accessible mail accounts and navigate only after one exact match; missing, ambiguous, and offline results remain retryable. Group-summary taps switch to the originating local account and open its inbox.
- Cleared future and already displayed calendar reminders on logout/account change, with a generation check so an in-flight schedule cannot recreate a canceled reminder.

## Relay contract and remaining signed-client gates

The observed production health response above advertises `previewMode: "sender-subject-snippet-v1"` without `routingVersion: 2`. The client sends `routingVersion: 2` only when health advertises it; existing signed v1 clients retain their original payload. The client can send `previews: true` on registration and fetch an exact message and current thread after a tap. Previously issued generic references without message identity cannot be retroactively mapped to an email. Sender, subject, and snippet pass through Expo and APNs/FCM and may appear on the lock screen according to device settings. A background or terminated rich notification already handed to a provider can appear after opt-out, switch, or logout, before JavaScript runs; relay revocation and dismissal cannot recall it. Relay opt-in limits new rich handoffs, and the app promptly revokes and dismisses on lifecycle changes. Signed device qualification and production activation follow the [production release checklist](zyndmail-production-release.md).

## Physical-device acceptance matrix

Run on a signed production-profile iOS build and Android build, with a non-sensitive company mailbox and a second authorized shared mailbox. Record build number, OS version, channel settings, event ID/time, relay registration/ref, provider receipt, device presentation and tap destination. Keep message content out of logs and screenshots used for incident reporting.

1. Fresh consent: deny, grant, later revoke in OS settings, token rotation, app reinstall; confirm status, renewal and no repeated permission prompt.
2. Delivery: personal and shared inbox; single and burst; unread/read elsewhere; junk filed at delivery; moved to trash before processing; duplicate or reordered events; offline arrival and reconnect.
3. Display: foreground, background and terminated; locked/unlocked; previews off; system preview hidden; Android generic and preview channel behavior; sound, vibration, badge, grouping and user-muted channel.
4. Tap: active account, other account, locked app, cold launch, shared account, deleted mail, expired ref, repeated tap, summary tap, and transient resolve/network failure.
5. Lifecycle: email switch off/on, preview off/on after a visible alert, staff-to-staff change, staff-to-personal change, logout, account removal, two devices with one revoked, lost-device server revoke.
6. Calendar: reminder fires when enabled; setting off; account change; logout; reminder already in tray; in-flight scheduling during change.
